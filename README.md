### The supplier worth calling first

In the synthetic procurement dataset, **94.5%** of Thames Industrial Supply’s order lines arrived late. The rate across all suppliers was **16.3%**. That gap gives a buyer somewhere specific to start.

[![Actual procurement dashboard preview. Cycles through spend overview, supplier analysis, and vendor risk. Open the repository.](./assets/studio/procurement-tour.gif)](https://github.com/IshtarthUmesh/Procurement-Performance-Dashboard)

**Procurement Performance Dashboard** · Python · pandas · Streamlit · Plotly · scikit-learn

Five interactive views explore **47,128 synthetic order lines and 106 suppliers**. The implementation compares like-for-like prices, estimates savings opportunities, and combines a weighted vendor scorecard with Isolation Forest anomaly detection.

Savings are addressable estimates from synthetic data, with stated assumptions.

### A flat line deserves a second look

The published hardware forecast for store 10 settles near **0.385 daily sales units** by day three. Does that tell a planner enough to make an inventory decision? Start by comparing it with the sales history.

[![Actual retail project outputs. Cycles between sales history and the 30-day forecast for store 10 hardware. Open the repository.](./assets/studio/retail-tour.gif)](https://github.com/IshtarthUmesh/Retail-Demand-Planning)

**Retail Demand Planning** · Python · pandas · statsmodels · Matplotlib

Select a store and product family, inspect stationarity, fit **ARIMA(1,1,1)**, and export **30 daily estimates**. The chart places the forecast beside the most recent **60 observations**.

Before operational use, I would evaluate held-out forecast error and compare against a simple baseline.

[**Open the repository ↗**](https://github.com/IshtarthUmesh/Retail-Demand-Planning) · [Read the model implementation](https://github.com/IshtarthUmesh/Retail-Demand-Planning/blob/main/demand_forecasting.py) · [Inspect the forecast CSV](https://github.com/IshtarthUmesh/Retail-Demand-Planning/blob/main/forecast_store10_hardware.csv)
